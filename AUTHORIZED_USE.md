# Authorized use

Use is restricted to environments and tasks explicitly authorized by the responsible institution. Professional access alone is not authorization to automate.

The first real task must be selected by the user. It must use a synthetic environment or synthetic record, be administrative and reversible, require human confirmation, have an easily verified postcondition, and involve no clinical decision, prescription, medical signature, deletion, financial operation, or irreversible transaction.

Never provide credentials to this project or Codex. Never bypass MFA or CAPTCHA, inspect cookies, tokens, headers, or network responses, capture patient screenshots, contact internal APIs, or send medical page content to GPT-5.6.
